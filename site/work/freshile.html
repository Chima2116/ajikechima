Freshile

An on-demand cleaning service, from choosing a service to paying for it.

Company
Jadara Innovations Ltd
Role
Product designer
When
2025
Scope
Website, user personas, customer and cleaner apps

What it is

Freshile lets people book a cleaner for upholstery, carpets, tiles and more. Pick a service, choose a cleaner, set a time and confirm.

The challenge

Booking a cleaner takes many small decisions: the service, who does it, when, where and how much. The risk was making it feel long, or hard to trust.

What I did

I designed the website, the user personas, and the customer and cleaner mobile apps. Cleaners show ratings and price up front, the booking summary keeps the total visible, and there is a short window to cancel after confirming. The admin dashboard is next.
