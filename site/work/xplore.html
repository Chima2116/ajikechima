A travel app that makes planning feel less overwhelming.

A mobile app concept that brings accommodation, flights and activities into one place.

Type
Personal project, not client work
Role
Designer
Scope
Mobile app: wireframes and high-fidelity screens
Year
2022

Problem

Planning a trip can be overwhelming and slow. The brief I set was an app that makes it easy to plan a trip, book accommodation and find things to do.

Who it is for

The persona is Jessica, a busy business consultant who adds leisure to work trips. She likes high-end hotels and fine dining and avoids crowded attractions. She is a persona, not a real person.

  • Easy booking of flights and accommodation for business trips
  • Finding upscale leisure activities that fit her interests
  • Fitting experiences around her schedule
  • Finding the best deals without a long search

Focus areas

  • Seamless planning: search and book stays, flights and activities in one place
  • Personalised: trips that match the user's interests
  • Time-saving: compare prices and images quickly
  • Clean, friendly design

High-fidelity design

Process and lessons

The process ran from problem identification through empathise, define, ideate, prototype and test. The main lessons were to research and test before committing to a design, to write a clear problem statement, and to iterate across several prototypes.
