About

Hi, I'm Chimaobi. I'm a product designer in Lagos, and I've been designing for 6 years, since 2020, when I was still in school.

Funny part: I studied Software Engineering. How I ended up a designer instead? By the grace of God. I found design in university, fell in love, and never went back.

The engineering degree wasn't wasted, though. I understand how developers think and work, so we get on well. Mostly. They will tell you I like to disturb them to make things look right. They are correct, and I'm not sorry.

I've been the creative kid since forever. I drew a lot, and I liked making something out of nothing. I also love solving problems, and design lets me do both at once.

Everything I design has to be perfect. I pick at UI, I pick at experience, I pick at the small stuff nobody asked me to look at. It's a bit of a problem, but you'll like the result.

These days I'm co-founding Nuton (opens nuton.app), a startup that helps people learn faster and understand more of what they study. I also talk to the people who use what I design, then watch them use the live product and change my design when I was wrong.

Outside work I'm very athletic. Football, volleyball, swimming, lawn tennis, table tennis, basketball. If it has a ball or a score, I'm playing it. I also play video games and watch anime, and yes, I'm goofy. I crack jokes at the wrong time.

Lagos is on West Africa Time (GMT+1), which overlaps almost the whole UK and European working day. I can work as an independent contractor or through an employer-of-record service.
